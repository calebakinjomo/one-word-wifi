One word describing my Wi-Fi — my entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72) one-word bounty. It lives in a single corner of the house, right next to the router, and refuses to come out anywhere else. Full bars in the hallway, nothing on the couch: a total recluse.

> Hermit

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) prints the entry word and writes it to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
